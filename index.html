Welcome to Bien Marko's

A calm private stay for rest, gatherings, and easy days by the pool.

Bien Marko's Pension House brings together practical comfort and a quiet tropical setting in Tabango, Leyte. Guests can book clean rooms, reserve cottages, plan small events, or enjoy a simple pool day with family and friends — all with warm, attentive local hospitality.

Fast, reliable Wi-Fi throughout the property

Swimming pool open daily for guests

Event spaces for gatherings & celebrations
